# Presenting the offline demo

1. Run `./scripts/start-demo.sh --model-rights-confirmed` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8877`.
2. Choose **Enter as Demo Director**. Show the Overview and local-only demo banner.
3. Open **Live Cameras** and start **Staged physical conflict — 1903 train film**. This is public-domain staged footage, not live surveillance.
4. Open its protected preview. Watch real suspicion scores and temporal confirmation. The measured incident appeared roughly 9 seconds after starting the source on this Mac.
5. Open the local notification and mark it read. Telegram delivery is separate and is not configured for this demo.
6. Review the incident. Use **Verify integrity** and **Play clip**. Explain that SHA-256 checks the preserved original; the model score is not a calibrated probability.
7. Choose **Acknowledge**, then **Confirm incident**, giving an observed reason. A human makes the decision. Refresh to show persisted state.
8. Show **AI Analytics** and **Activity Journal**. They use stored worker observations and audit records.
9. Start **Normal movement — historical greeting** and show its negative result. The sports control raises a false alert; disclose it rather than presenting the model as validated.
10. In **Settings**, choose **Reset demo**, review the confirmation, and confirm. Re-enter the clean run. Previous evidence remains archived privately.

All seven screens were checked at 390, 768 and 1440 pixels. A source stops at EOF in offline mode and stale preview frames are removed. Refresh preserves screen/incident targets. No bounding boxes, student identity claims or automated disciplinary action are produced.
